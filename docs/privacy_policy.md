# Privacy Policy for SpamSniper

**Effective Date:** May 25, 2026

## Privacy First

SpamSniper is built with privacy as a core principle.

SpamSniper does **not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal data or user information.

All spam detection, filtering, classification, and related processing are performed entirely on your device.

## Data Collection

SpamSniper does **not** collect any:

- Personal information
- Contact information
- Message contents
- Device identifiers
- Usage statistics
- Diagnostics
- Crash reports
- Analytics or telemetry data
- Advertising identifiers
- Location data
- Any other user data

## Data Processing

All app functionality operates locally on your device.

No data is sent to external servers for processing.

## Third-Party Services

SpamSniper does **not** use:

- Third-party analytics services
- Advertising SDKs
- Tracking services
- External data processors

## Advertising and Tracking

SpamSniper:

- Contains **no advertisements**
- Performs **no user tracking**
- Uses **no cookies, behavioral profiling, or advertising identifiers**

## Data Sharing

Because SpamSniper does not collect user data, there is no data to sell, share, or disclose to third parties.

## Local Storage

Any app settings or configuration required for functionality remain stored locally on your device and are never transmitted externally.

## Children's Privacy

Since SpamSniper does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If SpamSniper’s privacy practices ever change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

## Contact
